## Title

EdgeEver

## Short description

Private notes for your self-hosted EdgeEver workspace.

## Full description

EdgeEver is an open-source, self-hosted notes workspace built for fast capture, focused organization, and reliable access across devices.

Connect the app directly to your own EdgeEver instance and keep control of where your data lives.

• Browse notebooks, notes, tags, and attachments
• Search your local note mirror instantly
• Create and edit notes even when the network is unreliable
• Sync queued changes with your self-hosted workspace in the background
• Organize content with nested notebooks and tags
• Add images and file attachments
• Review note history and manage your workspace
• Use a clean mobile interface aligned with the EdgeEver web experience

EdgeEver contains no ads, analytics, tracking, or telemetry. The app connects directly to the instance you choose; the project maintainers do not operate a relay that receives or stores your notes.

An EdgeEver self-hosted instance is required. Learn more and deploy your own workspace at edgeever.org.
